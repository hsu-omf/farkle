Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Farkle Dice is designed to be played without creating an account with the developer. The app does not ask you to provide an email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or other similar personal information.

Farkle Dice stores game-related information locally on your device and uses limited third-party services for analytics, optional rewarded advertising, in-app purchases, and supported Game Center features.

Local Data

Farkle Dice stores game and app information locally on your device, including:

  • active and completed game information;
  • player-entered names;
  • game settings and preferences;
  • statistics and personal bests;
  • achievements and progression;
  • XP and Pips;
  • Daily Reward progress and streak information;
  • unlocked and selected Dice Skins;
  • selected themes and Cups;
  • purchase-entitlement state;
  • onboarding and other app-state information.

This locally stored information is not uploaded as a complete copy of your game data to the developer.

Selected gameplay and usage information is processed through Firebase Analytics as described below. Qualifying Solo leaderboard scores may also be submitted to Apple Game Center.

Analytics

Farkle Dice uses Firebase Analytics, provided by Google, to understand general app usage and improve the game.

The app may send limited product and gameplay information such as:

  • screens viewed;
  • game mode and game configuration;
  • target, opening, and minimum bank scores;
  • game completion and abandonment;
  • final game scores;
  • gameplay events such as Farkles, Hot Dice, banking decisions, risky-roll decisions, and scoring combinations;
  • achievement and progression events;
  • levels reached;
  • purchase-related product events;
  • rewarded-ad interactions;
  • review-prompt interactions.

Farkle Dice does not intentionally send player-entered names, email addresses, Apple IDs, Game Center identifiers or display names, precise location, custom user-entered text, or raw individual die-face results to Firebase Analytics.

Firebase Analytics may automatically process information such as an app-instance identifier, device and application information, session information, and approximate geographic information as part of its standard analytics operation.

Game Center

Farkle Dice uses Apple Game Center for supported gaming features, including Solo speedrun leaderboards.

When you use Game Center, qualifying Solo completion times may be submitted to Apple for leaderboard processing.

Farkle Dice stores your Game Center player identifier locally on your device to manage leaderboard submissions and avoid duplicate submissions. This identifier is not sent to Firebase Analytics or to a separate developer-operated backend.

Game Center authentication, player account information, leaderboard processing, and related services are provided by Apple and are subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices.

Advertising

Farkle Dice may offer optional rewarded advertisements after eligible completed games.

Watching a rewarded advertisement is optional. If you choose to watch one, the app may grant an additional eligible in-game reward after the advertisement is completed.

Rewarded advertising is provided through Google Mobile Ads. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device and app information, advertising-related identifiers where permitted, IP-derived approximate location, ad interaction information, and diagnostic information in order to provide, measure, prevent fraud in, and operate advertising services.

Where required, the app may display a consent or privacy-options interface through Google's consent-management services before advertising is shown.

Farkle Dice does not require rewarded advertisements in order to play the game.

The app does not intentionally use player-entered names or individual game results for advertising targeting.

Tracking and Advertising Identifiers

Farkle Dice does not intentionally use player-entered gameplay information for cross-app tracking.

The app does not request access to precise location for advertising.

Whether advertising-related identifiers or other advertising data are processed by Google Mobile Ads may depend on your device settings, consent choices, regional requirements, and Google's advertising services.

Purchases

Farkle Dice offers an optional non-consumable in-app purchase called Farkle Double Pip Rewards.

This purchase permanently doubles eligible gameplay-earned Pip rewards. It does not double XP, Achievement rewards, progression rewards, onboarding rewards, Daily Rewards, or other excluded rewards.

Purchases and purchase restoration are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit services. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details.

The app does not currently offer subscriptions.

Some Dice Skins, Cups, and other content may also be unlocked using Pips earned within the game. Pips are virtual in-game rewards and are not redeemable for cash.

Device Permissions

Farkle Dice does not request access to your:

  • contacts;
  • photos;
  • camera;
  • microphone;
  • precise location;
  • health data;
  • push notifications.

The app may use device features and Apple services necessary for gameplay and supported features, including audio, haptics, device language settings, internet access, Game Center, StoreKit, analytics, and rewarded advertising.

Third-Party Services

Farkle Dice currently uses:

  • Firebase Analytics, provided by Google, for app and gameplay analytics;
  • Google Mobile Ads, for optional rewarded advertisements;
  • Google User Messaging Platform or related consent services, where applicable, for advertising consent and privacy choices;
  • Apple Game Center, for supported leaderboard and gaming functionality;
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit, for in-app purchases and purchase restoration.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.
