Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Farkle Dice is designed to be played without creating an account with the developer. The app does not ask you to provide an email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or other similar personal information.

Farkle Dice stores game-related information locally on your device and uses limited analytics to understand app usage and improve the game.

Local Data

Farkle Dice stores game and app information locally on your device, including:

  • active and completed game information;
  • player-entered names;
  • game settings and preferences;
  • statistics and personal bests;
  • achievements and progression;
  • XP and Pips;
  • unlocked and selected Dice Skins;
  • selected themes and Cups;
  • onboarding and other app-state information.

This locally stored information is not uploaded as a complete copy of your game data to the developer.

Selected gameplay and usage information is processed through Firebase Analytics as described below. Qualifying Solo leaderboard scores may also be submitted to Apple Game Center.

Analytics

Farkle Dice uses Firebase Analytics, provided by Google, to understand general app usage and improve the game.

The app may send limited product and gameplay information such as:

  • screens viewed;
  • game mode and game configuration;
  • target, opening, and minimum bank scores;
  • game completion and abandonment;
  • final game scores;
  • gameplay events such as Farkles, Hot Dice, banking decisions, risky-roll decisions, and scoring combinations;
  • achievement and progression events;
  • levels reached;
  • review-prompt interactions.

Farkle Dice does not intentionally send player-entered names, email addresses, Apple IDs, Game Center identifiers or display names, precise location, custom user-entered text, or raw individual die-face results to Firebase Analytics.

Firebase Analytics may automatically process information such as an app-instance identifier, device and application information, session information, and approximate geographic information as part of its standard analytics operation.

Game Center

Farkle Dice uses Apple Game Center for supported gaming features, including Solo speedrun leaderboards.

When you use Game Center, qualifying Solo completion times may be submitted to Apple for leaderboard processing.

Farkle Dice stores your Game Center player identifier locally on your device to manage leaderboard submissions and avoid duplicate submissions. This identifier is not sent to Firebase Analytics or to a separate developer-operated backend.

Game Center authentication, player account information, leaderboard processing, and related services are provided by Apple and are subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices.

Advertising and Tracking

Farkle Dice does not display advertisements and does not use advertising networks.

The current iOS version does not access Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA), does not request App Tracking Transparency authorization, and does not implement cross-app advertising tracking or targeted advertising.

Firebase Analytics is used for product and gameplay analytics as described above.

Purchases

The current version of Farkle Dice does not offer real-money in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Some Dice Skins and other content may be unlocked using Pips earned within the game. These are virtual in-game rewards and do not involve real-money payment processing.

Device Permissions

Farkle Dice does not request access to your:

  • contacts;
  • photos;
  • camera;
  • microphone;
  • precise location;
  • health data;
  • push notifications.

The app may use device features and Apple services necessary for gameplay, such as audio, haptics, device language settings, Game Center, and internet access for analytics and Apple services.

Third-Party Services

Farkle Dice currently uses:

  • Firebase Analytics, provided by Google, for limited app and gameplay analytics;
  • Apple Game Center, for supported leaderboard and gaming functionality.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.
